How it works

  1. Image: open a photo (or paste / drop one) and position it inside the ring.
  2. Pop-out: choose the part that breaks out of the ring with Select, Brush or Auto.
  3. Ring: pick a Look, or build your own shape, colour and texture.
  4. Export: choose a size and download a transparent PNG. Tokens keep their editable project inside, so you can reopen them with Open.
